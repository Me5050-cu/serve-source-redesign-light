Why Community Networks Matter

The passion to serve already exists. The infrastructure often does not.

Serve Source helps local leaders organize shared visibility around the people, ministries, and organizations already serving their community. A Community Network gives each city or region a simple place to see who is serving, where they are located, what opportunities are available, and how people can connect.
